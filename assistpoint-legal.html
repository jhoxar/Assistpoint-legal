UNDERSTANDING THE PROGRAMS

CCM, RPM and PCM Serve Different Patient Needs.

The right program depends on the patient's conditions, clinical needs, payer requirements and the services actually furnished.

ONGOING COORDINATION

CCM

Chronic Care Management

What it is: Ongoing care management for patients with multiple chronic conditions. Under Medicare, CCM generally applies to patients with two or more chronic conditions expected to last at least 12 months or until death and that create significant health risk.

  • Comprehensive care planning
  • Ongoing non-face-to-face coordination
  • Medication and treatment follow-up
  • Coordination among providers and resources
  • Regular patient/caregiver support

Example: A patient with diabetes, hypertension and COPD who needs ongoing coordination across several providers.

CONNECTED DEVICE MONITORING

RPM

Remote Patient Monitoring

What it is: Collection and clinical use of physiologic data from a connected medical device. Medicare describes RPM for acute or chronic conditions using devices that digitally transmit data for review and treatment management.

  • Device setup and patient education
  • Connected physiologic data collection
  • Clinical review of transmitted information
  • Treatment-management communication
  • Potential earlier identification of concerning trends

Example: A patient with hypertension using a connected blood-pressure cuff that sends readings to the care team.

SINGLE COMPLEX CONDITION

PCM

Principal Care Management

What it is: Disease-specific care management focused on one complex chronic condition. CMS describes PCM as support for a single complex condition that can place the patient at risk of hospitalization, decline or death.

  • Disease-specific care planning
  • Focused coordination with specialists
  • Ongoing condition-management support
  • Medication and treatment coordination
  • Patient/caregiver communication

Example: A patient whose primary ongoing management need centers on one complex condition such as advanced heart failure.
